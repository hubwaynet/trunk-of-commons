Social dilemma games

Games about trust, fairness, taking advantage and reputation. Each one is a small model of a real situation: a shared resource, a group fund, a deal that only works if people keep their word. Play against strategy bots, watch them play each other, and learn to read the table.

Level 1 · Playable prototype

Rising Pool

Give to a shared pool as the stakes rise each round. Fall short together and everyone loses.

Level 2 · Planned

Centipede & Ultimatum

Keep building the pool, or grab it and decide how to divide it, then face the table's judgement.

Level 3 · In design

The Wheel

A turning wheel collects from some and rewards others. Can a table turn a trickle into a cascade?

TRUNK OF COMMONS

Educational social dilemma games about trust, cooperation, reputation and the commons. Designed by Jason Lee Lasky at hubway.net, Northern Rivers, NSW, from his design notes TrunK of Commons (2018) and the Wheel of Fortune Sharing Game (2017).

Draws on Robert Axelrod's iterated prisoner's dilemma tournaments and Elinor Ostrom's work on governing the commons.

Built with AI agents under human direction: code co-written with Claude (Anthropic), every change reviewed and published by Jason.
